Section 1: Official IELTS Task 2 Essay Prompt

Prescribed Stimulus
PLATE I: Historical Narrative Context — "The Weser River & The Pied Piper's Retribution" Hamelin Anno 1284
Formal Examination Mandate (For The Student)

In the story of 'The Pied Piper of Hamelin,' the Town Chief breaks his financial promise to the Pied Piper after the rats are drowned, leading to catastrophic consequences for the entire community.

"Write an analytical essay (250 words) investigating why the Town Chief broke his promise, what results occurred from his decision, and what moral lesson the townspeople learned. Organize your essay using Research Questions (RQs), Hypotheses (Hs), Evidence-Based Findings, and a Moral Justification."
Assessment Tier: Grade 8 ELA Advanced Task Genre: Literary Argumentative Inquiry Target Score: Band 4.0 / Band 9.0 Standard

Guiding Template: Step 1 RQ & Hypothesis Formulas
Research Question (RQ) Formula:

"This essay investigates: How did the Town Chief's refusal to honor his financial contract impact the safety and security of Hamelin?"

Hypothesis (H) Formula:

"It is hypothesized that the Chief's dishonesty directly caused the town to lose its children, proving that breaking promises destroys communal safety."

Section 3: Analytical Logic Map & Inquiry Strategy

Analytical Framework
Analytical Reasoning Chain: From Empirical Narrative to Ethical Rule
1. Story Evidence
  • Swarm of rats invading Hamelin homes and granaries
  • Chief offers 50,000 gold contract
  • Piper drowns rats in River Weser
  • Chief refuses payment: claims water & pipe did labor
  • Piper charms all children into mountain cave
2. Research & Hypothesis
  • RQ: Why did the Chief cheat the Piper & what resulted?
  • H: Dishonesty and greed destroy civic stability
  • Empirical comparison of breach vs catastrophic consequence
  • Analysis of fraudulent counterarguments
3. Moral Justification
  • Saving 50,000 gold pieces pale compared to lost progeny
  • Contractual honor is foundational to civilization
  • Retaliation confirmed hypothesis: deceit yields disaster
  • Universal rule: honor commitments without bad faith
